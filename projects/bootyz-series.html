Project / non-sport edition / front and back

BOOTYZ

One project expressed through a population of named front/back variants. The color names identify members of the edition; they do not name separate projects.

The parent project holds.

BOOTYZ is the stable project identity and absorption frame. Across the edition, its glyph, front/back relationship and compositional grammar remain connected while palette, tone and internal arrangements change.

Variation is the subject.

Unlike the UC Berkeley Football iteration of I’M COOL, BOOTYZ is not organized by sport, roster or institution. Its population develops through color-named inputs and edition behavior: Petaled Rose Tone, Soft Vanilla Accent, Ripe Strawberry Glow and the other labels describe individual variants inside the same project.
