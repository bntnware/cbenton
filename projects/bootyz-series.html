Project / deterministic edition system / front + back

BOOTYZ

One visual system capable of generating a large population of distinct front/back units. The eight images currently shown are samples from the machine—not the complete variation set.

The project is the generator, not the eight samples.

BOOTYZ begins with a stable two-sided object grammar and then changes through weighted sensory inputs. Thirty-two indexed channels feed chemistry, color, language, reaction, profile geometry, glyph allocation and adaptive layout. Each unit remains recognizably BOOTYZ while its internal state can change substantially.

A population, not a palette swap.

The production engine accepts packet sizes from 4 through 1,000 units in multiples of four. A 1,000-unit integrity test produced 1,000 unique names, 1,000 unique percentage vectors and 1,000 unique visible composite settings. This describes verified packet performance—not the limit of the system.

The combination of weighted channels, active subsets, curve families and tunable parameters, reaction states, generated language, chemistry placement, glyph allocation and adaptive layout creates a theoretical configuration space potentially numbering in the millions or billions. That space has not been reduced to one exact total because the generator includes continuous and parameterized choices.

01 / CHEMISTRY

Sensory inputs have literal chemistry.

The channel library links perceptual labels such as rose, citrus, fresh skin, earth, smoke and warm skin to explicit compounds and formulas. Those identities enter the rendered object through canonical molecule data and atom-accounted chemistry graphics; the scientific layer is structural data, not decoration.

02 / LINKED STATE

Variation occurs across connected layers.

A unit can change its weighted composition, active inputs, dominant notes, positive/negative balance, setting language, reaction state, chemistry placement, glyph allocation, profile marks, hash, barcode and adaptive arrangement. The visible result is a generated state of the system rather than a manually recolored composition.
