Object / two-sided / expandable

I’M COOL

A ticket-like object that began ambiguously, then navigated through iteration into a framework for integrating personal identity with agencies and institutions.

The object came first.

I’M COOL is not the name of a player series. It is the identity of the underlying two-sided object: a front and back held together by recurring language, registration, symbolic fields, identifiers and an evolving production grammar.

Its initial ambiguity was productive. The object did not begin as a conventional sports ticket or as an institutional commission. It developed by learning how outside identities could enter its structure without replacing it.

Integration, not rebranding.

Later iterations absorb the language, colors, data and affiliations of an institution or agency. The host identity becomes legible inside the object while I’M COOL remains the organizing architecture. Individual units can then vary extensively without becoming unrelated designs.

Object register

  • Form: two-sided ticket-like object
  • Origin: deliberately ambiguous
  • Development: iterative and rule-based
  • Capacity: individual, agency and institutional integration
  • Output: one object or a serialized population
01 / OBJECT

Front and back operate as one identity.

The two faces distribute different kinds of information but remain registered as a single object. Variation occurs within that relationship rather than through disconnected front and back designs.

02 / ITERATION

UC Berkeley Football

The UC Berkeley Football build is one institutional iteration of I’M COOL. The object expands across a 112-player roster while integrating Cal identity, player information, origin, position, identifiers and individual roster destinations.

Solomon Williams is one sample unit inside that population—not a separate project. The same is true of EJ Caminong, Jaron Keawe Sagapolutele, Emmanuel Okoye, Ian Strong and the other player-named samples.
