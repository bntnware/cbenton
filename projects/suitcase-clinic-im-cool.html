I’M COOL / service iteration / 32-unit edition

Suitcase Clinic

A serialized service-context iteration that integrates the visible Suitcase Clinic affiliation network into the two-sided I’M COOL object.

Return to the parent objectView selected units

The institution enters the object.

The edition retains the I’M COOL front/back architecture while incorporating the names and acronyms printed in the source: Suitcase Clinic, UCB, JMP, UCBSO, UFH, ASUC, TUC, Tuesday General Clinic and student-run service.

Rather than treating those affiliations as a detached list, the iteration distributes them through bearer language, article identity, service language and the visual field of the object.

Serial variation remains visible.

Thirty-two numbered units run from 0000-00 through 0000-31. Front glyph palettes, barcode-like marks, cube states, right-panel absorption graphics, binary article IDs and other details vary while the edition remains recognizable as one system.
