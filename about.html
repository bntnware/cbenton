About / Christopher Benton

I design the rules behind the image.

Graphic design, production engineering and visual storytelling meet in objects built for variation.

More than a look.

Typography, color, identifiers and physical constraints establish a visual grammar. Fixed elements create identity; variable elements create range.

The work begins in print: tickets, posters, cards, mailers and publications. Each system is developed through real formats, quantities and production constraints.

How I work.

I test typography, color, scale, sequencing and fabrication together. The result is a coherent family of distinct objects.

Capabilities

  • Visual identity systems
  • Variable-data and serialized design
  • Publication architecture
  • Campaign families
  • Print-ready production systems
  • Print imposition and production
