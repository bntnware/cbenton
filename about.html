About / Christopher Benton

I design the rules behind the image.

My work sits between graphic design, production engineering and visual storytelling. I build systems that carry identity across changing names, numbers, formats and editions.

More than a look.

A strong visual identity should survive contact with real production. It should know what stays fixed, what is allowed to change and how each new object remains part of the same family. That is the problem I like solving.

The work often begins in print—tickets, posters, cards, mailers or publications—but the underlying system can extend to campaign assets, web presentation and automated production.

How I work.

I move between close visual judgment and rule-based construction: testing typography, color, scale and physical constraints while building a repeatable architecture beneath them. The goal is not sameness. It is controlled individuality.

Capabilities

  • Visual identity systems
  • Variable-data and serialized design
  • Publication architecture
  • Campaign families
  • Print-ready production systems
  • Front-end presentation
